# AI Tools Nova — Channel Strategy

## Positioning (ek line)
"Roz 60 second me ek AI tool — Hindi me, bina jargon ke, seedha kaam ka."

Aapka differentiator English AI channels se: **Hinglish + Indian use-case**
(students, freelancers, small shop owners, job seekers) — yeh audience underserved hai.

---

## Pillar 1 — AI Tutorials (How-To) · 50% content
**Kyun:** Evergreen search traffic, affiliate revenue, high watch time.

**Format:**
- Shorts (45-60s): "1 tool, 1 problem, 1 result"
- Long-form (6-10 min): "Full workflow" — e.g. "AI se poora YouTube video banao, start to finish"

**Structure:**
1. 0-3s hook — result pehle dikhao ("Yeh poster maine 40 second me banaya")
2. 3-10s problem + tool naam
3. 10-45s steps (max 3 steps, screen recording)
4. 45-60s CTA — "Tool ka link description me"

**Monetization:** Tool affiliate programs (most AI SaaS ke paas hai), later apna
"AI Tools Nova Pack" (Notion/Sheet of 200 tools + prompts) ₹199 me.

---

## Pillar 2 — AI News & Updates · 30% content
**Kyun:** Fast growth, high CTR, subscribers wapas aate hain (habit).

**Format:** Shorts only, 40-50s, roz ya alternate din. Speed > polish.

**Structure:**
1. 0-3s — "GPT-5 ne abhi abhi yeh kar diya" (headline as hook)
2. 3-25s — kya hua, factually
3. 25-45s — **"Iska aapke liye matlab kya"** ← yahi differentiator hai,
   pure news channels yeh nahi dete
4. CTA — "Roz ki AI news ke liye subscribe"

**Rule:** News me speculation kam, source clear. Ek galat claim = trust khatam.

**Monetization:** Ad revenue + subscriber funnel (news se aaye log tutorials dekhte hain).

---

## Pillar 3 — AI Money-Making · 20% content
**Kyun:** Highest CTR aur shares — lekin sabse saturated. **Unique angle chahiye.**

### Aapke 4 unique angles (generic "earn $500/day" mat banao)

1. **"Real numbers, real proof"** — apna hi experiment dikhao.
   "Maine 30 din AI se Fiverr gig chalaya — ₹0 se ₹X. Poora data."
   Screenshots + failures bhi. Honesty = trust = shares.

2. **India-specific gigs** — "AI se local business ke liye Instagram content ₹3000/month me"
   — Indian pricing, Indian clients, UPI, GST. Foreign channels yeh cover nahi karte.

3. **"Debunk" series** — "Yeh AI earning method fake hai — maine try kiya."
   Yeh contrarian angle massive CTR deta hai aur credibility banata hai.

4. **Skill-first framing** — "AI se paisa nahi, AI se skill; paisa skill se aata hai."
   Har video me ek deliverable skill (thumbnail design, voice cloning, data cleaning).

**Rule:** Kabhi income guarantee mat do. "Yeh maine kiya, aapka result alag ho sakta hai."
YouTube ab misleading financial claims par demonetize karta hai.

## First 90 days plan

**Days 1-30 — Foundation**
- 30 Shorts (20 tutorials, 10 news), 4 long-form tutorials
- Goal: 100 subs, find kaunsa pillar chal raha hai (analytics dekho)
- Thumbnail + intro style lock karo (consistency > perfection)

**Days 31-60 — Double down**
- Jo pillar best CTR de raha hai uska 60% content
- 1 "flagship" long-form (15 min, deep) — yeh channel ka calling card
- Community tab active karo, poll daalo "next kis tool par video?"

**Days 61-90 — Monetize**
- 1000 subs + 10M Shorts views ya 4000 watch hours target
- Affiliate links lagao (jo tools already cover kiye)
- Digital product launch (tools pack / prompt library)

## Metrics jo dekhne hain (baaki ignore karo)
- **Shorts:** viewed vs swiped away (>70% good), avg view duration %
- **Long-form:** CTR (>4% good), avg view duration (>50% good)
- **Channel:** returning viewers % — yeh news pillar ka real score hai
- Har week: top 3 videos ka **pattern** nikaalo, agla week wahi format repeat

## Common mistakes se bacho
- Har video alag style — no. Consistency chahiye.
- Sirf tool list banana — "kaise use karna hai" batao, "kya hai" nahi.
- 100% AI voice + 100% stock footage = generic. Screen recording zaroor daalo.
- News me clickbait jhooth — ek baar caught, channel dead.
